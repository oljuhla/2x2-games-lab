Four tori of the 2×2 games

Each layer of the table wraps into a torus. Games with tied best payoffs join the four tori into one network.

How to read it

Each torus is one layer of the periodic table, 36 games. Going round the ring changes Row’s payoff pattern; going round the tube changes Column’s. Neighbouring dots differ by one swap of the lowest or middle payoffs: solid lines are 1↔2 swaps inside a tile, faint lines are 2↔3 swaps between tiles.

A 3↔4 swap moves a player’s best payoff and so jumps between layers. Each diamond is a junction: the game where both players tie their two best outcomes. Breaking its two ties gives four strict games, joined by Row’s and Column’s 3↔4 swaps.

Pipe junctions join one game on each of the four layers. Hotspot junctions join two games on each of two layers.

Colours

Games, names and swaps follow Bryan Bruns’ chart of Robinson and Goforth’s topology. Stacked bottom to top: layer 3 (win-win), layer 2, layer 4, layer 1. Equivalent tiles sit directly above one another, which is where the high swaps link them.
